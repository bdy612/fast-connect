Why Fast Connect?

Layered Encryption

Every connection is encrypted with AES-256, and each broadcast and direct message also passes through several layers of the Vigenère cipher.

FastAI Assistant

Chat with Google Gemini, have it describe images and videos, generate pictures, and turn text into audio.

Cloud Accounts & Friends

Sign up once, here or in the app. Your password is stored only as a salted hash, and your profile and friends list follow you to any computer.

Host Your Own Server

Start a chat server in one click, or connect to an existing one. The networking is multi-threaded and keeps connections stable.

Ready in about two minutes

Download it, double-click the installer, and click Install. It sets everything up and adds a shortcut. No typing needed.
